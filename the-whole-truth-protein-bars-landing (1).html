Got questions?

Questions before you choose?

Here are the practical questions people tend to ask before picking a protein bar. Check the individual product page for the exact SKU you are considering.

Can I have a protein bar if I do not work out?

Protein bars can also fit into everyday snacking and on-the-go routines, not only workout sessions. Use the nutrition and ingredient panel on the specific bar to decide what fits your needs.

How much protein does a bar provide?

Protein amounts can vary by range and SKU. The individual product page is the best place to confirm the exact protein quantity before you buy.

Are these bars vegetarian?

The Pro bars featured here contain whey protein concentrate, which is dairy-derived. Check the ingredient list on the exact product page for the bar you choose.

What should I know about whey and lactose?

Because whey comes from milk, people avoiding dairy or managing a dietary concern should review the exact ingredient and nutrition information for the chosen SKU.

Are any of the featured bars vegan?

The Pro bars featured on this page use whey protein concentrate and therefore are not vegan. For other ranges, check the ingredient list on the individual product page.

Do these bars contain sugar?

“No added refined sugar” is not the same as “sugar-free.” Dates are used in the featured bars, so check the full nutrition panel for the exact sugar content of the SKU you want.

Who makes these bars I’m buying?

The brand shares manufacturing information, including facility and FSSAI licence details, on its official manufacturing page and connects the protein-bar range to the Bada Barkhana story.
